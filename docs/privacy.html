Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

This is a personal, single-user tool ("autopost") used by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we handle

  • OAuth access/refresh tokens for the owner's own accounts, stored encrypted in a private GitHub Actions configuration. They are used only to upload the owner's videos.
  • The videos, captions and hashtags the owner chooses to publish.

What we do not do

We do not collect, sell, share or analyse personal data of any other person. We do not track visitors of this page. Data is not shared with third parties other than the platforms the owner publishes to (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) and an AI text service used to draft captions from the video title and tags.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time in the security settings of each connected account, and delete the stored tokens by removing the repository secrets.
